Test Croquet & Mallet Sports Club

The club

We've been playing on the three lawns at Riverside Park since 1967. Around eighty members, all welcome — competitive players, social players, and absolute beginners. We play Association, Golf, and Ricochet. Equipment supplied. The clubhouse opens an hour before play for a cuppa and a chat.

Where

12 Riverside Drive, Riverbank QLD 4560 (Parking available on Park Road, behind the bowls club)

When we play

Monday 1pm — Association Croquet Wednesday 9am — Golf Croquet Friday 1pm — Ricochet Saturday 2pm — Social play (all codes) Summer hours apply Oct–Apr (start 30 minutes earlier).

Come and try

First Tuesday of every month, 10am. Free. Equipment provided. Wear flat shoes and bring a hat. We'll teach you the basics in about 90 minutes.

Fees

  • Visitor green fee: $5 First Come & Try: free Full membership: $140 / year (concession $90) Coaching: free for members, $20 / session for non-members
