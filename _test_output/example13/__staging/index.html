Test Croquet & Mallet Sports Club

Three lawns by the river, eighty members, open six days a week

Plan Your Visit

We've been playing on the three lawns at Riverside Park since 1967. Around eighty members, all welcome — competitive players, social players, and absolute beginners. We play Association, Golf, and Ricochet. Equipment supplied. The clubhouse opens an hour before play for a cuppa and a chat.
