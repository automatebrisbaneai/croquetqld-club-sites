New Players Welcome

Come and try for free

Your first session costs nothing. No experience, no special gear. Pick a session and turn up.

Just arrive at any session. Someone will welcome you, explain the game, and get you on a lawn within a few minutes. Mallets, balls, and hoops are supplied — bring flat-soled shoes and a hat.

About the Club

A welcoming club

Competitive players and social players both belong here. Closed Thursdays; all other days, just turn up.

When to Come

Session Schedule

Closed Thursdays. All other days, just turn up to the session that suits you.

Monday 1 pm — Association Croquet Tuesday 9 am — Golf Croquet Tuesday 3 pm — Social Wednesday 1 pm — Ricochet Friday 9 am — Golf Croquet Friday 3 pm — Social Saturday 1 pm — Association Croquet Sunday 9 am — Golf Croquet

Social sessions run from 2 pm in winter (April to September). All other session times are year-round.
