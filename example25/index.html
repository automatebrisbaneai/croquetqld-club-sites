About the club

Point Lookout Croquet Club has played on the foreshore lawns since 1948. The site looks across the mouth of the Brisbane River — old fig trees, well-kept turf, a clubhouse that's seen most of our members through three generations. About 65 of us meet through the week. We're a friendly bunch — competitive when we want to be, social the rest of the time. Beginners and visitors are always welcome.
