About the club

A club with weight

Our club has run continuously since 1962. Fifty members across four lawns. Tournaments, social roll-ups, coaching for newcomers — all of it serious, none of it solemn. If you've played before, you'll find a club that meets the standard. If you haven't, you'll find people glad to teach you.
