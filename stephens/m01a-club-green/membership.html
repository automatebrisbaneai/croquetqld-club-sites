Membership

Playing times and fees for Association Croquet and Golf Croquet at Stephens Croquet Club.

Association Croquet players

Meet on Wednesday mornings and Saturday afternoons.

Playing times - Wednesdays: 9.45am - Saturdays: 12.45pm

Golf Croquet players

Meet on Friday and Sunday afternoons.

Playing times - September to April: 3.00pm - May to August: 2.00pm

Membership fees

All members pay an annual club membership fee. This annual fee includes green fees, insurance, affiliation fees and participation in club championships. It may be paid in full or by instalment.

  • Casual green fees, registered players from other clubs: $10.00
  • Casual green fees, visitors and unregistered players: $15.00
Your first sessions are free
