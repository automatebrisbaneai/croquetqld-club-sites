Contact

Stephens Croquet Club welcomes new members and casual visitors all year round. Our clubhouse is also available for hire for meetings, corporate events and celebrations.

Looking to get in touch?

For bookings, membership or anything else, phone the club and your call will reach the right person.

0413 732 986

Email: stephens@croquetqld.org

Post: PO Box 545, Annerley QLD 4103

Stephens Croquet Club on Facebook

The club is run entirely by volunteers. The committee: President, Vice President, Treasurer, Secretary, Committee, Captain, Association Croquet, Captain, Golf Croquet and Come and Try coordinator.

Find us at Yeronga Park

Yeronga Park Mallet Sports Centre, 104 Frederick St, Yeronga (access via Frederick St off Villa St).
