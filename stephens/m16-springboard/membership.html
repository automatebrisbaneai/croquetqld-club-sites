Play times and fees

Association Croquet meets on Wednesday mornings and Saturday afternoons. Golf Croquet meets on Friday and Sunday afternoons.

The club week

  • WednesdayAssociation Croquet9.45am
  • FridayGolf Croquet3.00pm (September to April)2.00pm (May to August)
  • SaturdayAssociation Croquet12.45pm
  • SundayGolf Croquet3.00pm (September to April)2.00pm (May to August)

Golf Croquet starts an hour earlier from May to August. Visitors are welcome on any play day.

One fee a year, then play as often as you like.

All members pay an annual club membership fee. This annual fee includes green fees, insurance, affiliation fees and participation in club championships. It may be paid in full or by instalment.

Questions people ask

Do I need to book?

No. Come on any play day and a member will look after you.

Which game should I play?

Golf Croquet is the quickest to learn. Many members play both, and you can try each before you choose.

What does membership cover?

Your green fees, insurance and the club championships, for the whole year.

Who do I ask about joining?

Phone the club on 0413 732 986.
