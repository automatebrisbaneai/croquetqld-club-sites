Our stories

What the club has been up to: games, results, visitors and birthdays. Each story opens on HOOPLA, the Croquet Queensland news site.

28 October 2026

Discover the history of the Stephens Shire

The Annerley-Stephens History Group Inc has an event on 7th November at the Yeronga Community Centre. It's a chance to learn more about what shaped our local area, originally the Stephens Shire.

24 October 2026

Why play croquet? A message from our President

Our President shares why she loves croquet, from the friendships to the tactics. The club thanks Jacky for the eye-catching result.

15 October 2026

Stephens Croquet Club celebrates 103rd birthday

Stephens Croquet Club turned 103 with Golf Croquet doubles, a sausage sizzle and birthday cake. Deb and Geraldine won the 4th Horse and Plough event.

28 September 2026

Join us for Tuesday evening croquet in Yeronga

Perfect conditions are predicted for Tuesday evening croquet at the Stephens club. Join us for four courts, friendly knowledge sharing, and a great community atmosphere.

24 September 2026

Tuesday evening social croquet under the lights

Stephens Croquet Club invites players to join Tuesday evening social croquet sessions, now lit by three years of court lighting.

23 September 2026

Stephens members shine at CAQ Division 3 and 4 Medals

Two Stephens members enjoyed a weekend of friendly competition at the Croquet Queensland Division 3 and Division 4 Medal events held at Bundaberg Croquet Club.

14 September 2026

Stephens wins GC Pennant as Windsor takes AC Metropolitan title

Stephens Croquet Club defeated Twin Rivers to win the GC Division 2 Pennant, while Windsor Croquet Club claimed the AC Metropolitan Pennants at Stephens, with the home team finishing as runners-up.

11 September 2026

Stephens Croquet Club receives grant for new linemarker

Stephens Croquet Club thanks Club Yeronga for a grant used to purchase a new linemarker for the club.

30 August 2026

Graceville wins the Annual Patron's Cup over Stephens

Graceville Croquet Club secured the Annual Patron's Cup in a close contest against Stephens, finishing with 10 games to 8.

27 August 2026

Stephens Division 2 Golf Croquet Team Wins Close Pennant Match

The Stephens Division 2 Golf Croquet team secured a narrow victory against Wynnum Croquet Club last Sunday, finishing with a net hoop of 53-48 after five games.

19 August 2026

Stephens hosts CAQ State Championships Doubles

Stephens Croquet Club hosted the Doubles event of the CAQ State Championships, with 20 teams competing over two days.

16 August 2026

State Gateball Championships held at Stephens Croquet Club

The CAQ State Gateball Championships took place at Stephens Croquet Club this weekend, with 10 teams competing in glorious weather. Rockhampton Croquet Club claimed the Lola White Shield.
