Membership

Association Croquet meets on Wednesday mornings and Saturday afternoons. Golf Croquet meets on Friday and Sunday afternoons.

Come and try, it's free

Club play days and start times
WednesdayAssociation Croquet9.45am
FridayGolf Croquet3.00pmSeptember to April2.00pmMay to August
SaturdayAssociation Croquet12.45pm
SundayGolf Croquet3.00pmSeptember to April2.00pmMay to August

Golf Croquet starts an hour earlier from May to August.

Membership feesOne fee a year.

All members pay an annual club membership fee. This annual fee includes green fees, insurance, affiliation fees and participation in club championships. It may be paid in full or by instalment.

Casual green fees
Casual green fees, registered players from other clubs$10.00
Casual green fees, visitors and unregistered players$15.00

Casual green fees are paid per visit.
