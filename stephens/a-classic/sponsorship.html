Sponsorship

Why sponsor us

Stephens Croquet Club has been part of the Yeronga community since 1923, over a century of bringing local people together on the lawns at Yeronga Park. We are a not-for-profit community club run entirely by volunteers, and we welcome new members and casual visitors all year round. A sponsorship helps us keep the courts in good condition, run Come and Try sessions for newcomers, and keep membership affordable for people of all ages.

What a sponsor gets

  • Your logo and a link on the events diary on our home page.
  • A listing on this sponsorship page.
  • A mention at club events and functions held at the clubhouse.
