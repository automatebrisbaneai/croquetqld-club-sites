About the club

A note from the green

We've played croquet on these greens for many years. The four lawns sit at the back of the park, behind the jacarandas. About fifty of us meet through the week — competitive players, social players, and a steady stream of people who came along once and stayed. Everything is provided. The lawns are maintained. The kettle is on by 9.30.

When we play

Monday 1pm — Association Croquet Wednesday 9am — Golf Croquet Saturday 2pm — Social play Visitors always welcome on Saturday.
